Nardi — Support

Long Nardi for iPhone

Contact

For questions, bug reports or feedback, email nardi.master0@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Reporting a bug

Please include your app version (shown in the App Store listing or on your device under Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Nardi), your iPhone model and iOS version, and what happened just before the problem. A screenshot of the board helps a lot for rule or move questions.

Game data

Nardi can upload anonymous records of finished games to help improve the computer opponents. You can turn this off at any time under Settings → Share anonymous game data. See the privacy policy for exactly what is collected.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. To ask for uploaded game records to be deleted, email us with your approximate play dates.
