Nardi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

What we collect

When a game finishes — by being played out or by resignation — Nardi uploads a record of that game: the dice and moves of every turn, who moved first, the result, whether it ended by resignation, the kind of opponent (which computer level, or a human on the same device), the computer opponent's model version, the app version and build, and the time the game finished. Games you quit before the end are not uploaded.

How it's anonymous

The first time the app uploads, it creates a random anonymous ID for your installation. Your games are stored with that ID so they can be grouped together, but it is not tied to your name, email, Apple ID, device name or any other personal information, and we never ask for any. Deleting the app discards the ID; a reinstall gets a new one.

Why

To understand how the game is played so we can improve it: measure and tune the computer opponents, find rule bugs, and study things like first-move advantage.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share game data, use it for advertising, track you across other apps or websites, or combine it with data from other sources.

Where it's stored

Game records are stored with our database provider, Supabase (supabase.com), which processes them on our behalf. As with any internet service, Supabase may briefly log the network address a request came from; we do not use it to identify you.

Your choice

You can turn off Settings → Share anonymous game data at any time. Games finished while it is off are never uploaded — not even if you turn it back on later. Your match history on the device keeps working either way.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: nardi.master0@gmail.com. Include your approximate play dates; because the data is anonymous we may not be able to identify your games without the in-app ID.
